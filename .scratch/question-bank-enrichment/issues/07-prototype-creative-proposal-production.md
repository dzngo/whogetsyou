# Prototype Creative Proposal Production

Type: prototype
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 01, 02, 03, 05

## Question

What concrete agent roles, inputs, creative operations, and collaboration pattern produce genuinely varied question proposals rather than paraphrases or convergent ideas? Prototype the proposal side of the pipeline for both approved Question Aspect/Perspective combinations and the stricter path that proposes new Question Aspects or Question Perspectives.

## Comments

The interactive logic prototype is captured on branch `codex/prototype-creative-proposal-production` at commit `efdd918` in `prototypes/creative_proposal_factory.html`.

It contrasts independent scouts with a shared brainstorm, delays question wording until concept comparison, limits scout input to Level, ontology, coverage needs, and compact bank fingerprints, and blocks new Question Aspects or Perspectives from question production until the stricter taxonomy path approves them.

## Answer

The approved topology is recorded in [`creative-proposal-production.md`](../creative-proposal-production.md). The user validated all prototype decisions.

Aspect, Scenario, Perspective, and Contrast scouts independently create structured Creative Concepts without seeing peer ideas or full bank questions. Concept overlap is removed before one composer writes one question per survivor. A global novelty screen follows wording, and proposal production ends at an evidence-rich handoff to independent evaluators. Newly proposed Aspects or Perspectives cannot produce trusted questions until the stricter taxonomy process approves them.
