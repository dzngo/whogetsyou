# Define Automatic Taxonomy Evolution

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 02, 04, 06, 08, 09, 10

## Question

How may the Question Enrichment Pipeline automatically propose, distinguish, admit, merge, split, deprecate, or reject Question Aspects and Question Perspectives without allowing taxonomy bloat, synonym proliferation, or a self-reinforcing collapse in variety? Define the stricter evidence path and its relationship to existing bank entries.

## Answer

Taxonomy evolution applies only to Aspects and Perspectives and is temporally separated from question generation: a proposed term cannot seed questions or count self-generated evidence. Candidates require repeated independently created unclassified concepts, explicit definitions and counterexamples, whole-taxonomy neighbor comparison, isolated kind/distinctness/reusability/clarity/variety checks, adversarial absorption attempts, and shadow classification. The decider may Add, Map to existing, Apply an existing-taxon change, Reject, or abstain to human review. Aliases do not become active taxa. Renames, merges, splits, and deprecations create a new Taxonomy Version, preserve historical IDs, reclassify every affected question, and cannot release automatically while any affected classification remains uncertain.
