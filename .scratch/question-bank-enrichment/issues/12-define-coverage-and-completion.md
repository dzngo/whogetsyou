# Define Coverage and Bank-Completion Policy

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 01, 02, 06, 10, 11

## Question

Once the quality contract, diversity ontology, and evolving taxonomy are known, what coverage model and stopping rules make the Question Bank sufficiently large and varied? Decide which combinations are meaningful, how gaps and overrepresented regions are measured, what minimum depth is healthy, how diminishing returns are recognized, and why the resulting target is evidence-based rather than an arbitrary raw count.

## Answer

Use a versioned Coverage Plan over meaningful Named Theme + Level + Aspect + Perspective regions, classifying combinations as Required, Exploratory, or Invalid instead of filling the full Cartesian product. A Required region initially needs three questions with different Semantic Scenarios and non-uniform Answer Spaces. Theme membership pressure never affects classification, and multi-theme questions count once globally. Completion requires every Theme × Level horizon to be healthy, no unresolved concentration defects, and three independent forty-concept discovery rounds with two proposal strategies that each yield under five percent accepted distinct questions and no new valid regions. Low yield from weak agents or quality failures is not saturation. Counts are reported, but raw size cannot declare completion.
