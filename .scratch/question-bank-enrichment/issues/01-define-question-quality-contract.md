# Define the Question Quality Contract

Type: grilling
Status: resolved

## Question

What qualities must every Canonical Question satisfy before it can enter the Question Bank, which qualities are hard gates versus graded signals, how do those rules differ between Shallow and Deep, and what concrete examples define the boundaries of answerability, specificity, personality revelation, round utility, delight, theme fit, emotional safety, and weak or generic output?

## Answer

The approved contract is recorded in [`question-quality-contract.md`](../question-quality-contract.md).

Shallow questions may be simple generic polls and are not required to reveal a distinctive personal characteristic; Deep questions must invite a personally characteristic but emotionally safe answer. Every question must pass understandability, ordinary answerability, Bounded Openness, realism, Level, safety, structural, Canonical-English, and global bank-distinctness gates. Natural forced choices are allowed, humor is optional, and variety outranks polish after the minimum gates pass.

Questions are generated for exactly one Level without a required Theme, compared against the whole bank, and then assigned zero or more equal Theme Memberships. Random is a wildcard rather than a membership. **Prototype the Diversity Ontology with Real Questions** subsequently resolved global Question Aspect as the bank classification concept while retaining Theme-owned Question Angle only as a legacy generation aid.
